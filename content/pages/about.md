I'm Eduardo Castilho, and I go by **reapr** in security. I'm specializing in digital forensics and incident response, and this site is my public lab notebook: what I build, what I investigate and what I learn along the way.

## What gets published

- **Notes.** Short entries on artifacts, techniques and lab exercises as I study them.
- **Writeups.** Longer pieces with an ID: lab case studies (`LAB`), research (`RL`) and malware analysis (`MW`). Each one states its question, environment, method, observations and limitations.
- **Tools.** Small open-source tools built for the lab work, with documentation.

## How the work is done

Everything is developed independently, in lab environments I build myself, with public datasets and public samples. Nothing here comes from real incidents, customers or any employer's systems or data.

I try to keep four things apart in every writeup: what was observed, how I interpret it, what remains a hypothesis, and how confident I am. Tool versions are pinned so results can be reproduced. If you find a mistake, I want to know.

Feedback, corrections, collaboration on research and coordinated disclosure are welcome. I don't offer commercial services.
